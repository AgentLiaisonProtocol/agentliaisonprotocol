# Licenses

Three licenses apply, each to its own paths. The specification is an open standard; the reference broker is source-available. Where this summary and a license differ, the license governs.

| What | License | What it lets you do |
|---|---|---|
| The specification text (`spec/`) | [CC BY 4.0](../../LICENSE-SPEC) | Implement ALP from it without asking, and use, give away or sell your implementation, a broker included, on the license’s terms, such as attribution. It grants no patent license. |
| The JSON Schemas and the conformance vectors (`schemas/`, `vectors/`) | [Apache-2.0](../../LICENSE) | The same, for implementations built from them. Apache-2.0 includes a patent license, on its terms. |
| The specification tools, the site build, the website’s stylesheet and script, and the bridge kit’s code, like any other code outside `reference/` | [Apache-2.0](../../LICENSE) | Apache-2.0, as for the schemas and vectors. |
| Other documentation: the bridge kit, the announcement, the website’s pages, figures and walkthrough text, and the project’s documents | [CC BY 4.0](../../LICENSE-SPEC) | CC BY 4.0, as for the specification text. |
| The reference broker (`reference/`): its source, tests, migrations, deployment files and demo | [FSL-1.1-ALv2](../../reference/LICENSE.md), licensed by Lucaryin LLC | Use, modify and run it for your own organization; not make it available to others in a commercial product or service that substitutes for it or offers substantially similar functionality. Each version becomes Apache-2.0 two years after it is first published. |

## Implementing ALP

Anyone can implement ALP from the specification, schemas and vectors, and use, give away or sell their implementation, a broker included, without asking. Implementing it needs no agreement with Lucaryin, and neither does using, running or modifying the reference broker for any purpose its license permits. The licenses grant those rights, on their terms, such as attribution.

Code copied or adapted from `reference/` stays under the reference broker’s license until the version it came from becomes Apache-2.0.

## The reference broker

The reference broker is source-available under the Functional Source License, Version 1.1, ALv2 Future License (FSL-1.1-ALv2). It is free for any organization to use, modify and run for itself. Its license defines a “Competing Use” and lists the purposes it permits, such as internal use, non-commercial education and research; the [README](../../README.md#license) quotes those terms.

## Patents

CC BY 4.0 grants no patent license; Apache-2.0 and FSL-1.1-ALv2 each include one, on their terms. Before version 1.0, the steward will decide on a patent commitment for the specification text, for example by adopting a specification license that includes one.

## Contributions

Contributions are accepted under CC BY 4.0 or Apache-2.0, as [Contributing](../../CONTRIBUTING.md) says; contributions to the reference broker are distributed with it under FSL-1.1-ALv2. The name “Agent Liaison Protocol” names the specification, and no trademark is claimed in it.
