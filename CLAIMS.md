# Claims and their evidence

Every claim the [README](README.md), the [announcement](announcement/introducing-agent-liaison-protocol.md), the [bridge kit](bridge-kit/) and the website’s hand-written pages ([site/pages/](site/pages/), including the overview at https://agentliaisonprotocol.org) make about the Agent Liaison Protocol (ALP), with the evidence for it: a section of the [specification](spec/alp-v0.1.md), a [conformance vector](vectors/), a test or demo step of the [reference broker](reference/), a vendor page listed in [bridge-kit/SOURCES.md](bridge-kit/SOURCES.md), the test with real agents on November 7, 2026 ([bridge-kit/README.md](bridge-kit/README.md#tested-with-real-agents)), or the test against a live Microsoft Entra tenant on November 8, 2026 ([SELF-HOST.md](SELF-HOST.md#tested-against-a-live-entra-tenant)); the records of these two tests aren't in this repository. A row marked **limit** states what the claim does not cover. Checked on November 8, 2026, against this repository: every cited file, section and demo step, with the tests and demos passing. "Approvals page" is the person's page on their broker (the specification's web view). The rows under [Poppy](#poppy-the-personal-agent-protocol) cover the [ALP and Poppy page](https://agentliaisonprotocol.org/poppy/) too, and were checked on November 20, 2026.

The walkthrough on the overview and on the page of four exchanges (https://agentliaisonprotocol.org/docs/walkthroughs/) restates the specification’s worked examples (Appendix A) step by step; its evidence is the specification and the vector files each step names.

Test files are under `reference/src/`; "demo step N" is a step of `npm run demo`.
